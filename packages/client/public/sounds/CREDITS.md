# Sound credits

## Sound effects

All effects except the coins in `sell.mp3` are original: synthesized from scratch for Arcane Academies by
`packages/client/scripts/synth-sfx.mjs` (no samples, no third-party material), and dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). One palette for all of them: D-major tuning, glass
and bell timbres, paper/air noise and the same small "stone hall" reverb. Mono mp3, 80 kbps.
