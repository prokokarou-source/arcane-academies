# Sound credits

## Sound effects

Picked by ear by the owner: tap, sell, turn, tick, duelWin and join are from the new set; select, build, tower, clash, duelLoss and start are kept from the first (Kenney, CC0) set, and victory/gameOver swap the two first-set jingles. The new-set effects (except the coins in `sell.mp3`) are original: synthesized from scratch for Arcane Academies by
`packages/client/scripts/synth-sfx.mjs` (no samples, no third-party material), and dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). One palette for all of them: D-major tuning, glass
and bell timbres, paper/air noise and the same small "stone hall" reverb. Mono mp3, 80 kbps.
